## Relevance observation kept separate from a defect

Before the replacement-fixture handoff, one broad question about what the test document included returned an explicit “not enough information” abstention plus four unrelated source citations from KB Kỹ thuật. The assistant did **not** answer with unsupported business facts. Because the question was broad and the handoff later established a replacement fixture, this is not counted as a C01/C02 failure or a confirmed relevance bug. The query/source behavior is visible in `luna-chat-answer1.png` and `luna-chat-sources.png`; the cited source opened an unrelated document in `luna-chat-source-open.png`. Sol can decide whether this exploratory observation merits a separate retrieval query after reviewing the exact source and replacement-fixture evidence.

On the Business graph, the rendered graph reported 37 links while the visible “Thực thể trọng tâm” degree badges read `0`; node hover/focus worked. This is recorded as an observation for Sol to inspect, not declared a defect from UI evidence alone.
